# Price sourcing rules (read by the daily re-verification)

1. Prefer the brand's own product page (jackery.com, anker.com / ankersolix.com, us.ecoflow.com, etc.) for any product price. Use the price shown now, including a live sale.
2. If the brand price needs a coupon code, note "with code" next to the price.
3. Use third-party sources (homegridhq.com, homebatteryreport.com) only if the brand page shows no price or the product is discontinued there.
4. Every price gets a source URL and an "as of" date (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. If a product is sold out on the brand site, keep the price and say "sold out".
